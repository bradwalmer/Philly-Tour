Philadelphia in brief

  1. 1682 William Penn founds the city as the center of his Pennsylvania colony.
  2. 1776 The Declaration of Independence is adopted in the Pennsylvania State House.
  3. 1787 Delegates draft the U.S. Constitution in the same building.
  4. 1829 Eastern State Penitentiary opens and becomes a model for prisons worldwide.
  5. 2003 The Liberty Bell Center opens in Independence National Historical Park.
